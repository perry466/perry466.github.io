The second sample post. It exists to verify list ordering, pagination, and the adjacent-post links at the bottom of an article.

## What it checks

- The date column lines up with the title in the post list
- The archive groups posts by year
- Tag pages count posts correctly
- No empty modules appear when there are no related posts

## Sample text

Placeholder copy fills the layout so you can see whether leading, paragraph spacing and heading hierarchy still hold once there is more text on the page.

> Delete every sample post and the home page shows an empty state instead of a blank screen.
