Six mornings, one experiment: start at 06:30 and see what changes.

## What actually happened

- The first hour is quiet in a way no other hour is. No messages, no reason to check anything.
- By 10:00 I had already done the day's hardest task, which made everything after it feel optional.
- By 21:30 I was useless. Not tired exactly, just out of words.

## What did not happen

I did not become a morning person in the sense people mean it. I still have to force myself out of bed, and I still lose the argument about twenty percent of the time.

> The gain is not extra hours. It is that the hours you do have are spent on purpose.

## The thing I will keep

Not the 06:30 part. The part where the day's one important task happens before the inbox exists. That can be done at any hour, and it is the only piece that clearly worked.
